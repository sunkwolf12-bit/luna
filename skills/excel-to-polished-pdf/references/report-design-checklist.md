# Report Design Checklist

Use this checklist after inspecting the workbook and again before delivery.

## Structure

- Does the first page identify the document, person/client/course, date, and major sections?
- Are section pages titled with enough context to stand alone?
- Are large tables split across pages instead of over-shrunk?
- Do continuation pages repeat a title or continuation label?
- Are formulas, assumptions, and interpretations visually separate from data tables?

## Tables

- Are headers dark or otherwise clearly distinguished?
- Are row labels wide enough to read?
- Are large numbers compacted or formatted consistently?
- Are totals, final results, net income, EBIT, UAI/UAII, balances, and status rows emphasized?
- Are negative values, zeroes, percentages, and currency values formatted intentionally?
- Are columns aligned by data type?

## Typography And Spacing

- Is the smallest body/table text still readable?
- Are line heights comfortable enough for accents and descenders?
- Are note boxes wrapped without clipping?
- Is there enough margin for printing?
- Is the page visually balanced without huge unused areas?

## Content Integrity

- Are all sheets or explicitly relevant sheets represented?
- Are all obvious sections represented?
- Are side notes, formulas, interpretations, and extra comments included?
- Did encoding artifacts get cleaned without changing meaning?
- Are calculated values taken from cached workbook values when possible?
- Did the agent flag contradictions instead of silently rewriting analysis?

## Verification

- PDF file exists and has nonzero size.
- Page count is plausible.
- Page size/orientation fits the content.
- Extracted text includes expected titles/sections.
- Visual render or browser/PDF preview was checked when available.
- Final answer links only the final PDF unless the user asks for support files.
